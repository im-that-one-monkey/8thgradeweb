Why Lebron is the GOAT

I can name a million reasons why

1] Lebron.....plays basketball

  • Played it longer than anyone else
    1. 1607 regular season games
    2. 292 playoff games
    3. 72,560 minutes, statmuse.com
  • Played it better than anyone else
    1. Never averaged under 20 PPG
    2. 43,127 points, 51,434 including playoffs
  • Played it cooler than anyone else
    1. He looked hot while doing it

2] He's a top tier leader

  • Had to put tragic teams on his back
    1. '07 Cavs -- Carried them to the Finals in his 5th season in the league
    2. '15 Cavs -- Took the 67 win Warriors to 6 games by himself
    3. '16 Cavs -- Took down the winningest regular season team of all time in the only 3-1 comeback in NBA History
    4. '18 Cavs -- Went on the greatest individual playoff run in NBA history to carry a team where his second option averaged 11 points per game and dropped 51 on the greatest team ever assembled just for JR Smith (see gallery for photo) to sell the bag
    5. '26 Lakers -- Carried them past the first round as a 41 year old in a season where he has been the 3rd option
  • Some things he did that Michael Jordan didn't that truly embodies his leadership and mentoring
    1. Play on the same team as his son
    2. Not punch his teammates
    3. Not have a son go to jail

3] The most notorious records Lebron has set

  • Longevity based
    1. 1,297 consecutive games with 10 or more points. Next closest Michael Jordan with 866
    2. 23 seaons played in the league (and still elite!)
  • Dominance based
    1. The only player in NBA history with a 40 point game on all 30 teams
    2. He has won 42 playoff series; that is more than 24 active teams (or 80% of the league) have in their entire history
  • Just some crazy stuff
    1. Spent more years of his life in the league than out of it
    2. 15% of the league wasn't even born when Lebron got drafted
